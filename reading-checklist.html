Focus

Get your brain ready.

Look at the cover and the title. Why did the author write this book? Tap your best guess.

Easy as PIE!

Now ask yourself:

  • What do I already know about this?
  • What do I think I will find out?

Fluency

Warm up your reading voice.

Read the first page out loud. Try to read it:

  • ✓SmoothlyLike you are talking, not like a robot.
  • ✓CarefullySay the words that are really on the page.
  • ✓With feelingMake your voice match the story. Stop for a moment at the end of each sentence.

Was it bumpy? Read it again. It gets easier every time.

Losing your place? Slide your finger under the words as you read.

Read & Highlight

Read your book until the timer stops.

Highlight a word when:

  • You can't say it
  • You don't know what it means

Stuck on a word? Try these tricks.

Can't say it?

  • Sound it out. Say each sound slowly. Then squash them together.
    sh · i · p → ship
  • Spot a pattern. Look for bits you already know.
    shchtheaaioa-ing-tion
  • Chop it up. Break a long word into small parts.
    un · help · ful

Don't know what it means?

  • Read around it. Read the words before and after. Look for clues.
  • Swap it. Put in a word you know. Does the sentence still make sense?
  • Still stuck? That's OK! Leave it highlighted and ask a grown-up later.
