Chore Quest

How to play

Kids earn stars for helping at home, then spend them on rewards.

  1. 1Pick your nameTap your name at the top. Your chores match your age.
  2. 2Do a chore, then tap itFinish the job for real, then tap its card. It gets a Done! sticker. Tapped by mistake? Press Undo.
  3. 3Collect starsStars raise your level. Do at least one chore every day to grow your streak and win badges.
  4. 4Spend themIn the Reward shop below, press Redeem when you have enough stars.

For grown-ups: set up in 2 minutes

  • Press Grown-ups (top right) and answer the quick times-table question.
  • Add each child with their age. Remove the sample players Mia and Leo.
  • Set rewards and their star prices to suit your family.
  • Optional: turn on grown-up check so stars only count after you approve a chore, or add your own chores.
  • Daily quests reset every night and weekly quests reset on Monday. On a birthday, update the age so your child moves up a stage.
